# Roadmap

The six-week curriculum ends with a split-replica remote-worker runtime.

Week 2 defines TP=2 on one host. Week 3 routes to Pods without preserving the GPU shape.
Week 4 scales Pods without defining whether TP changes. Weeks 5 and 6 add multi-node
runtime and FabricFleet.

Week 6 reuses Week 4 references #1-3.
